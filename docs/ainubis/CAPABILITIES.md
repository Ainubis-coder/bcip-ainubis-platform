# Ainubis CRM Intelligence Capabilities

This document lists the active and future capabilities of the Ainubis CRM Intelligence engine.

## Active Capability: `CRM_PIPELINE_HYGIENE`
Evaluates opportunity velocity and hygiene parameters:
1. **Idle Time Checks**: Flags opportunities with zero activity logged for over 14 days.
2. **Overdue Follow-up Checks**: Alerts managers when open tasks associated with the deal are past due.
3. **Forecast Completeness Checks**: Observes missing target close dates or zero estimated values and recommends updates.

## Planned Capabilities

### `CRM_DATA_QUALITY`
Audits contact records for missing email addresses, incomplete phone numbers, or unverified trustee fields.

### `CRM_LEAD_QUALIFICATION`
Analyzes inbound leads against historical customer profiles and scores qualification readiness.

### `CRM_DUPLICATE_DETECTION`
Uses contact name similarity indexes to flag potential duplicate customer registrations.

### `CRM_CLIENT_RELATIONSHIP_INTELLIGENCE`
Aggregates activity touchpoints to gauge client satisfaction and account health.
