# BCIP Platform Specification Overview

## 1. Product Purpose
The Body Corporate Intelligence Platform (BCIP) provides comprehensive, multi-tenant digital infrastructure for the sectional title and property management industry.

## 2. Multi-Tenant Role Hierarchy
* **Platform Super Admin**: Global platform operations, tenant provisioning, system monitoring.
* **Managing Agent Admin**: Executive control over the managing agent organisation and all assigned scheme portfolios.
* **Portfolio Manager**: Day-to-day operations across a cluster of assigned body corporate schemes.
* **Scheme Trustee**: Fiduciary oversight of a specific body corporate, financial approvals, AGM participation.
* **Property Owner**: Owner of sections/units, levy account statements, maintenance reporting, voting.
* **Resident Tenant**: Occupant reporting maintenance and receiving community announcements.
* **Contractor**: Vetted service vendor receiving work orders and submitting completion evidence.
* **Ainubis Agent**: Autonomous AI services executing monitoring, recommendation, and notification jobs.

## 3. Platform Modules (Phased Roadmap)
* **Phase 1**: Project Bootstrap & Foundation (This Phase)
* **Phase 2**: Multi-Tenant Hierarchy, Schemes, Properties & CRM
* **Phase 3**: Finance, Invoicing, Levies & Maintenance Workflows
* **Phase 4**: Governance, AGMs, Documents & Integrations
* **Phase 5**: Ainubis AI Intelligence Layer & Autonomous Agents
* **Phase 6**: Hardening, Production Deployment & Compliance Audits
